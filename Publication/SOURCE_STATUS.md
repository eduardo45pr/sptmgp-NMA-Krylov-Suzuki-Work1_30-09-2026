# Paper 1 source status

**Paper 1:** *A Reproducible Software Workflow for Compact Pauli Analysis of Molecular Vibrational Hamiltonians*

This repository contains:

- the original manuscript PDF;
- the revised manuscript PDF;
- the original Supplementary Material PDF;
- the revised Supplementary Material PDF;
- `main_addition.tex` - insert-ready LaTeX for the new quantum-resource / viewer section;
- `supplement_addition.tex` - insert-ready LaTeX for the Supplementary Material additions;
- compilable PDF-wrapper `.tex` files;
- the quantum-viewer architecture figure in PDF and PNG.

Important: the original editable native `main.tex` for Paper 1 was **not supplied** in the source materials used to assemble this release. Therefore the package does not pretend that `main_addition.tex` is the full original manuscript source. When the original Paper 1 `.tex` is available, merge `main_addition.tex` and `supplement_addition.tex` into that native source.

The revised PDFs are the canonical current manuscript files in this repository.
